# User Research Plan
## Status
No interviews are documented yet. The questions below are a plan, not completed research.
## Interview questions (3–5 potential users)
1. How do you track food now?
2. What is the most inconvenient part?
3. What is hardest to estimate: ingredients, portion, calories, macros?
4. Would you start a food entry with a photo?
5. What confirmation/explanation would you need before trusting a suggestion?
6. Which summary is useful?
7. What privacy concerns do meal photos or diet history raise?
8. What would make you stop using a food diary?
## Record template
Participant code (not name); date/method; consent; observation; pain point; evidence strength; interpretation; design implication.
Label findings as Observed, Interpretation, Assumption or Decision. Do not fabricate interviews. If formal interviews are not possible, discuss an approved alternative with the supervisor and clearly state the limitation.
