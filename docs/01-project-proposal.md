# Project Proposal — NutriSmart
## Title
Development of an Intelligent System for Monitoring and Analyzing Dietary Intake.
## Problem
Maintaining a consistent food diary and estimating nutritional values can be time-consuming. Photo-assisted logging may reduce manual effort, but food identity and portion size are uncertain and require user confirmation.
## Target users
Adults who want to record meals and compare estimated nutrient totals with self-selected targets. The system is not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions.
## Aim
Design and evaluate a mobile nutrition-tracking system combining food logging, nutrient calculations, and transparent rule-based insights, with a planned photo-assisted workflow.
## Objectives
1. Review research on food recognition, portion estimation, dietary tracking and usability.
2. Gather user needs and distinguish findings from assumptions.
3. Compare existing systems and technology alternatives.
4. Define measurable requirements and acceptance criteria.
5. Design system context, use cases and component architecture.
6. Build a UX prototype and minimum working Android application.
7. Test calculations, persistence and core user scenarios.
8. Document limitations, risks and future work.
## Scope at checkpoint
Proposal, requirements, comparison tables, architecture diagrams, clickable UX prototype, evaluation plan, Android starter and project backlog.
## Expected outcome
A documented prototype for food logging, nutrient totals, goals and explainable insights. The photo flow in the current UX prototype is simulated and does not run a trained recognition model.
## Assumptions to validate
Users want faster meal logging; they will confirm suggested foods and portions; food values can be sourced reliably; transparent insights are useful.
## Risks
Nutrition data may be inaccurate; photos do not directly reveal portion mass/calories; time is limited; health recommendations require careful validation.
